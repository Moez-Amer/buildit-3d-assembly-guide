Questions

Which furniture works?

Flat-pack furniture that comes with a drawn, step-by-step manual: shelves, wardrobes, chests of drawers, desks and chairs. Most flat-pack brands draw their manuals the same way. Products with motors or electronics are not supported.

How accurate is it?

Not perfect yet. Parts lists usually come out right; the 3D steps are right more often than not, but some will be wrong. Buildit marks the steps it is unsure about, and you can open the matching manual page next to each step. When the two disagree, follow the paper manual.

What is early access?

Scanning your own manual is not open to everyone yet. Create an account now and we'll email you once when it opens. Nothing else, and no payment until then.

Do I need an account?

Not to try the example. You need one to scan your own manual, so your guides are kept for you. You sign in with a link sent to your email. There is no password to remember.

What happens to the pages I upload?

They are sent to an AI model that reads the drawings, and kept with your guide so you can compare each step with the original page. When you delete a guide or your account, its pages are deleted too.

Is Buildit made by a furniture company?

No. Buildit is independent and not connected with any furniture brand or shop.

Use your own Anthropic API key
  • Used for this one scan, then forgotten. Never saved, never written to a log, never shown to anyone.
  • It goes over an encrypted connection to our server, which uses it only to send this scan to Anthropic.
  • Anthropic bills the scan to your account: usually $0.20–2.50 per manual.

Safest: in the Anthropic Console, make a new key just for Buildit, set a spending limit, and delete the key after your scan. Then it is useless to anyone, us included.

    Tips for a good scan

    • Lay the manual flat in good light, with no shadows on the drawings.
    • Fill the frame with one page, phone held straight above it.
    • Include the parts and hardware pages. They give the part letters and counts.
    • Pages can be in any order. Duplicates are fine.
