Tips for a good scan

  • Lay the manual flat in good light, no shadows on the drawings.
  • Fill the frame with one page, phone held straight above it.
  • Include the parts and hardware pages. They give the part letters and counts.
  • Pages can be in any order. Duplicates are fine.
